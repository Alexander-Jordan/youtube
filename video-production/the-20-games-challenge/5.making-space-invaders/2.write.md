## Script

### Intro

#### Short history

> Do you recognize this game?
> You're not alone.
> Since its release back in 1978, Space Invaders took the arcades, and then the world, by storm.
> Today, it's considered one of the most influential and greatest video games of all time.

#### The 20 games challenge

> I'm part of a challenge named "The 20 games challenge", in which you recreate 20 existing games.  
> In this video, I'll show you how I remade Space Invaders in Godot.  

### Planning

> For the last 2 games, I started the projects by filling in a GDD.
> But recently I learned more about rapid prototyping.
> So instead of defining the game too quickly, I will try to:
>
> 1. Implement
> 2. Test
> 3. Tweak
> 4. Repeat
>
> This saved me a lot of time!
> And as prep, I only created small GitHub issues with the instructions directly from the 20 Games Challenge page.

### Core game components

#### Main level and ship

> After creating the project and setting it up, I made a main dark scene with 4 invisible walls.
>
> Then I started to build the player ship.
> I used a sprite-sheet I found online with all the sprites I needed for the game.
> And you can control the ship using a mouse or a finger on touchscreen devices.

#### Aliens

> In the original there were 3 types of aliens: Octopus, Crab, and Squid
>
> To spawn them I made a script called `AlienRow`, which were responsible for 1 row of aliens.
> This was the first version of a small, reusable component that will be named `Spawner`, and together with a `Spawnable` it handles spawning and despawning.
>
> When the game starts it will spawn 5 rows, with 11 aliens on each row, just like the original.
> They will start moving right, and change direction when they collide with a wall, moving one step closer to the player each time.

#### Shooting

> To shoot the aliens I made a few more reusable components.
> The Aliens have a `Destructable`.
> So when a `Destructor` enters it, some damage is dealt and destroy the `Destructable`.
> The bullets contains both a `Destructor` and a `Projectile`, which is extending the `Spawnable` component.
> When the player shoots, the `Projectile` is spawned and stored by a `Spawner`.
> Thanks to components like these, it's easy to make the Aliens shoot back, just by adding the ones I need.

### UFO

> Based on a random timer, a red UFO should appear and cross the screen above the aliens.
> Just like the aliens, the UFO has a `Spawnable` and a `Destructable`.
> And I made a super small custom extension of the `Spawner`.
>
> So pretty quickly I had a bonus UFO appearing randomly.
> And I could even spawn in as many as I like with a dev-tool I added, pretty cool!

### UI, health, and score

> Time for the UI!
> I added a score, best score, and life counter.
> If the player dies, a restart button appears.
> And if the player clears the screen, it resets after a few seconds.
>
> The player also lose instantly if the aliens reach the player ship.

### Final touches

> After fixing some bugs (like this one [ALIENS GOES DOWN TO THE PLAYER REALLY FAST]), I added a main menu.
> I also added SFX and music.
> And lastly I added an icon, a splash screen, and exported the game.
